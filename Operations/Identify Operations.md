
Task 1: Identify Operations

OP-Id | Operation                        | Purpose 

OP-01 | Perform Sensor Self-Check        | Check that essential sensors are working.

OP-02 | Check Control Devices            | Check that environmental-control devices are working.

OP-03 | Record Artifact Information      | Record the artifact identification information.

OP-04 | Load Environmental Profile       | Load the required environmental limits.

OP-05 | Monitor Environment              | Monitor temperature and humidity continuously.

OP-06 | Check Door Status                | Check whether the chamber door is open or closed.

OP-07 | Correct Temperature              | Restore temperature when outside the allowed range.

OP-08 | Correct Humidity                 | Restore humidity when outside the allowed range.

OP-09 | Verify Environmental Conditions   | Confirm corrected conditions are within limits.

OP-10 | Detect Vibration                 | Detect significant vibration in the chamber.

OP-11 | Handle Power Failure             | Switch to emergency power or safely shut down.

OP-12 | Verify Safe Artifact Removal      | Confirm the chamber is safe before removal.
